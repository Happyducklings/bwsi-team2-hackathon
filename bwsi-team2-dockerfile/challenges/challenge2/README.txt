Want to open the file in a decompiler on your own computer? Open a NEW terminal
on your machine (not this one), go to the game's "bwsi-team2-dockerfile" folder,
and run:

    ./download.sh 2

The files here (e.g. msg) will be saved to downloads/level2/ so you can load
them into your decompiler.
