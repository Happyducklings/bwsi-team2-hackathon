Need to see the picture on your own computer? You can't open an image inside
this text terminal. Open a NEW terminal on your machine (not this one), go to
the game's "bwsi-team2-dockerfile" folder, and run:

    ./download.sh 1

The files here (e.g. OSINT1.png) will be saved to downloads/level1/ so you can
open the image and reverse-image-search it.
